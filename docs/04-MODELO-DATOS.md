# 04. Modelo de Datos y Reglas de Negocio

## 1. Entidades Principales (PostgreSQL)
- **`profiles`**: `id` (UUID, PK = `auth.users.id` ON DELETE CASCADE), `display_name` (1..100 chars), `avatar_path`, `role` (`user` | `admin`, default `'user'`), `created_at`[cite: 2, 14, 25]. Implementada en `supabase/migrations/20261110000001_phase2_profiles_and_rls.sql` con trigger `on_auth_user_created` (`public.handle_new_user()`) y bloqueo de escalación de rol (`public.prevent_profile_role_escalation()`).
- **`subjects`**: `id` (UUID), `owner_id` (FK `profiles` ON DELETE CASCADE), `title` (1..120 chars), `description` (<= 1000 chars), `visibility` (`private` | `public`), `status` (`draft` | `published` | `archived`), `published_at`, `created_at`, `updated_at`[cite: 4, 14, 15]. Implementada en `supabase/migrations/20261110000002_phase3_subjects_topics_and_rls.sql` con trigger `enforce_subject_publication_rules` (solo `admin` publica en catálogo público en v1).
- **`topics`**: `id` (UUID), `subject_id` (FK `subjects` ON DELETE CASCADE), `parent_topic_id` (FK opcional a `topics` ON DELETE CASCADE), `title` (1..120 chars), `description`, `sort_order` (>= 0), `created_at`, `updated_at`[cite: 14]. Implementada en `supabase/migrations/20261110000002_phase3_subjects_topics_and_rls.sql` con trigger recursivo `enforce_topic_hierarchy_integrity` (misma materia y prevención de ciclos).
- **`documents`**: `id` (UUID), `owner_id` (FK `profiles` ON DELETE CASCADE), `subject_id` (FK `subjects` ON DELETE CASCADE), `topic_id` (FK opcional a `topics` ON DELETE SET NULL), `storage_path` (UNIQUE), `filename` (`*.pdf`), `mime_type` (`application/pdf`), `size_bytes` (`> 0 AND <= 15728640`), `status` (`uploading` | `available` | `failed` | `unavailable`), `created_at`[cite: 14]. Implementada en `supabase/migrations/20261110000003_phase4_documents_and_storage_rls.sql` con trigger `enforce_document_integrity` y bucket privado `study-documents`.
- **`questions`**: `id` (UUID), `topic_id` (FK `topics` ON DELETE CASCADE), `author_id` (FK `profiles` ON DELETE CASCADE), `type` (`single_choice` | `true_false` | `multiple_select`), `prompt`, `explanation`, `difficulty` (`easy` | `medium` | `hard`), `status` (`draft` | `published` | `archived`), `source_document_id` (opcional, ON DELETE SET NULL), `source_document_name` (opcional, preserva nombre ante borrado de PDF), `source_page` (opcional >= 1), `version` (int >= 1), `created_at`, `updated_at`[cite: 4, 5, 14, 17]. Implementada en `supabase/migrations/20261110000004_phase5_questions_and_options_rls.sql` con trigger `enforce_question_integrity`.
- **`question_options`**: `id` (UUID), `question_id` (FK `questions` ON DELETE CASCADE), `text`, `is_correct` (boolean), `feedback`, `sort_order` (>= 0)[cite: 14]. Implementada en `supabase/migrations/20261110000004_phase5_questions_and_options_rls.sql` con políticas RLS delegadas en `can_read_question` y `can_write_question`.
- **`quiz_sessions`**: `id` (UUID), `user_id` (FK `profiles` ON DELETE CASCADE), `subject_id` (FK `subjects` ON DELETE CASCADE), `mode` (`practice` | `challenge` | `mistakes`), `settings_json` (JSONB con `seed`, `questionCount`, `questionIds`), `status` (`in_progress` | `completed` | `abandoned`), `started_at`, `completed_at`, `score` (>= 0), `accuracy` (0..100)[cite: 6, 14]. Implementada en `supabase/migrations/20261110000005_phase6_quiz_sessions_and_attempts_rls.sql` con trigger `enforce_quiz_session_integrity`.
- **`attempts`**: `id` (UUID), `session_id` (FK `quiz_sessions` ON DELETE CASCADE), `user_id` (FK `profiles` ON DELETE CASCADE), `question_id` (FK `questions` ON DELETE CASCADE), `response_json` (JSONB), `is_correct` (boolean), `elapsed_ms` (>= 0), `answered_at`, `question_version` (int >= 1), `UNIQUE(session_id, question_id)`[cite: 14]. Implementada en `supabase/migrations/20261110000005_phase6_quiz_sessions_and_attempts_rls.sql` con trigger `enforce_attempt_integrity` (bloquea respuestas en sesiones ajenas o cerradas `SESSION_CLOSED`).
- **`xp_events`**: `id` (UUID), `user_id` (FK `profiles`), `event_key` (UNIQUE para idempotencia), `event_type`, `points`, `reference_id`, `created_at`[cite: 6, 14].
- **`user_daily_activity`**: `id` (UUID), `user_id` (FK `profiles`), `activity_date`, `questions_answered`, `minutes_studied`, `UNIQUE(user_id, activity_date)`[cite: 14].

## 2. Reglas de Corrección y Validación
- **`single_choice`**: Mínimo 2 opciones y exactamente 1 correcta[cite: 17]. El ID enviado debe pertenecer a la pregunta y coincidir con la opción correcta[cite: 17].
- **`true_false`**: Respuesta booleana/opción fija definida; comparación exacta[cite: 5, 17].
- **`multiple_select`**: Al menos 1 opción correcta y 1 incorrecta[cite: 17]. El conjunto de IDs seleccionados debe coincidir exactamente con el conjunto correcto[cite: 5, 14, 17].
- **Visibilidad pública:** Solo se listan en el catálogo público los registros con `visibility = 'public' AND status = 'published'`[cite: 4, 15].
